# SMGD DriveLog – Terms and Conditions

SMGD DriveLog is a private, self-hosted trip journal (mileage log). Each installation is run by
a vehicle owner on their own server, for their own use. It is not offered to the public as a
service and has no other end users than the owner.

## Use of vehicle data

After the owner has signed in with their own Volvo ID and given consent, DriveLog reads data
about the owner's own vehicle through the Volvo Cars APIs: odometer, fuel and battery level,
trip statistics, engine status, charging state and last reported position. Access is
read-only. DriveLog never sends commands to the vehicle.

The data is used for one purpose: to compile a trip journal that the owner uses for their own
mileage reporting.

## Consent

The owner can disconnect the vehicle in DriveLog at any time (Integrations → Volvo) and can
withdraw the consent with Volvo Cars. Once disconnected, DriveLog stops reading vehicle data.
